# Store trademarks

Store logos identify linked services; they do not imply endorsement.

- Xbox, GOG.com, EA and NVIDIA: Simple Icons v11.15.0, CC0-1.0.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons
  Only the foreground fill was adjusted to match the existing store assets.
- Gaijin: https://gaijin.net/assets/img/svg/logo-gaijin.svg
  Copyright and trademark remain with Gaijin. Used to identify its account connection.
- Steam, Epic Games, Ubisoft Connect and Battle.net: added with the first Qt client
  commit (86a40e4e) without a recorded source. They follow the Simple Icons (CC0-1.0)
  drawings of each brand's mark, but no Simple Icons release from v5.0.0 to v16.0.0
  matches them exactly. Rasterised at 256 px they differ from those releases by
  2.4 % of pixels for Steam, 0.5 % for Ubisoft and 0.4 % for Battle.net (v10.0.0 and
  later). The Epic Games mark differs by 6.2 % because it has no "GAMES" wordmark.
  The fill is #E8F2EA. Copyright and trademarks remain with Valve, Epic Games,
  Ubisoft and Blizzard Entertainment.
